Offline-First Architecture

Mental Math Speed Training was architected from the ground up to empower users to practice arithmetic privately, securely, and seamlessly even without an active internet connection:

  • No Account Sign-up: You do not need an email, username, or password to access math practice modes, speed runs, or test labs.
  • On-Device Storage: All exercise questions, personal best records, training streaks, accuracy metrics, and user configuration settings (vibration, audio volume, practice reminders) are written strictly to your device's sandboxed storage (Hive / SharedPreferences).
  • Zero Central Answer Logging: We do not operate an external database that records or transmits the math calculations or specific answers You enter in the App.

Device Permissions

Mental Math Speed Training requests minimal device permissions, strictly required to provide its functional features:

  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS):
    Enables local reminders for your daily mental math training at the specific time of day chosen by You. All notification alarms are scheduled and triggered 100% locally on your device without transmitting data externally. You can enable or disable reminders anytime within the App's Settings.
  • Network Connection (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE):
    Utilized exclusively by the Google AdMob SDK to load advertisements and verify active network status.
  • Haptics & Vibration (VIBRATE):
    Triggers subtle tactile haptic feedback during keypad entries, correct answers, or timer alerts to improve training rhythm and immersion.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because your math training data is stored exclusively on your device:

  • Data Retention: Your training history, records, and settings remain on your Device for as long as the Application remains installed.
  • Data Deletion: You have full control. You can permanently delete all local game data at any time by going to your device's Settings → Apps → Mental Math Speed Training → Storage → Clear Data / Clear Storage, or simply by uninstalling the Application.

Children's & Minors' Privacy

Mental Math Speed Training is an educational math tool suitable for students, teenagers, and adult learners who wish to sharpen their calculation agility.

The Application is not directed specifically at children under 13 years of age (or the relevant age threshold under applicable local jurisdiction), and We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

If You are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has inadvertently provided Us with personal data, please contact Us immediately. We will promptly take necessary steps to inspect and remove such data.
